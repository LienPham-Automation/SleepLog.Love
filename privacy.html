Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Sleep Log · sleeplog.love · Last updated: 21 August 2026

This policy explains what information Sleep Log ("we", "us") collects and how we use it. It also includes an important disclaimer about the guidance in the app. Please read both.

Who we are

Sleep Log is operated by [Your name or business name]. For any privacy question or request, contact us at [your-contact-email].

What we collect, and what we don't

  • Your email address, only when you choose to enter it to start using the app, and only after you tick the consent box.
  • Your baby's sleep & feed logs stay on your own device. They are saved in your browser (local storage) and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. If you clear your browser data or use another device, they don't come with you unless you use the in-app Backup/Restore.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies. The only browser storage we use is what the app needs to save your logs and settings on your device.

Why we use your email (lawful basis)

We use your email, with your consent, to ask for your feedback, send occasional baby-sleep tips, and share updates or offers about the app. You can withdraw consent at any time (see below) and this does not affect anything before you withdrew it.

Who processes your email

When you submit your email, it is delivered to us through Web3Forms (a form-delivery service acting on our behalf). We may also use an email marketing provider (for example MailerLite) to store subscribers and send emails. These providers process your data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until it's no longer needed for the purpose above.

Your rights

If you're in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email [your-contact-email]. Every marketing email also includes a one-click unsubscribe link. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

The app is intended for use by parents and caregivers (adults). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Information you record about your baby stays on your device and is not collected by us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.

⚠️ Important disclaimer: not medical advice

Sleep Log is an informational tool, not a medical device or a medical service. The wake-window / nap-timing suggestions and sleep-duration ranges are general guidance only (broadly aligned with the American Academy of Pediatrics and National Sleep Foundation) and every baby is different. Nothing in the app is a substitute for professional advice.

Always follow your baby's cues and consult your pediatrician, GP, or health visitor for medical questions or concerns about your baby's sleep, feeding, or health. Do not rely on this app for any decision affecting your baby's health or safety. Use of the app is at your own discretion.
